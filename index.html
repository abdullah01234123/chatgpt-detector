IMPORTANT TO KNOW

AI detection is an estimate, not a verdict

No automated detector can reliably prove who or what wrote a passage. Editing, paraphrasing, short samples, and writing style can affect results. Do not use a detector score alone to make academic, employment, or disciplinary decisions.

HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

Can this tool detect ChatGPT text?

The planned detection service will look for patterns associated with AI-generated text, including text that may have been produced by ChatGPT. Results are estimates and are not guaranteed to identify the author.

Can an AI detector be 100% accurate?

No. AI detection systems can produce false positives and false negatives. Treat any score as one signal among several, not definitive evidence.

Does this page currently analyze text?

This is the front-end preview. A detection API and secure server-side connection still need to be configured before live analysis is available.

Should I paste private or confidential information?

Do not submit sensitive or confidential material unless the final service's privacy policy clearly explains how text is processed, retained, and shared.
